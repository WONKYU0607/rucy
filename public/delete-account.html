Account Deletion

Australopithecus Idle RPG · Developer: Wonkyu Kim

Delete in the game

  1. Sign in to the game (the app or rucy.vercel.app) with your Google account.
  2. Open the menu (☰, top right) and tap Delete Account.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm. If you signed in a while ago, Google may ask you to confirm once more.

If you can't open the game

Email dnjsrb980607@gmail.com from, or mentioning, the Google account you use in the game. We will verify the request, delete your data without delay, and let you know.

What is deleted

  • Your Google sign-in account record (email and account ID)
  • All game progress (waves, currencies, gear, skills, allies, etc.)
  • Your ranking entry and nickname

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. We do not keep any of this data afterwards. Server access logs kept by our hosting and cloud providers are handled under their own policies. See the Privacy Policy.
